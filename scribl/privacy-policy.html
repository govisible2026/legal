Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

Scribl is a personal notes app. All of your notes stay on your own device. We do not collect, transmit, store on servers, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no ads.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Scribl mobile application ("Scribl", "the app", "we", "us"), published by govisible. It explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Scribl has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.

The notes you create — including their titles, content, dates, color tags, and pinned status — are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This content never leaves your device, is never sent to us or to any third party, and is not accessible to us.

3. How your data is stored

  • All notes are saved locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage.
  • We operate no servers and keep no copy of your notes.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your notes are permanently deleted from your device. Because we hold no backup, this cannot be undone.

4. Permissions

Scribl does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or files. It does not require an internet connection to work and does not transmit data over the network.

5. Third-party services

Scribl contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No usage data, identifiers, or crash data are shared with any third party by the app.

6. Children's privacy

Scribl does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Data security

Because your notes never leave your device, their security depends on your device's own protections (such as your screen lock and device encryption). We recommend keeping your device secured with a passcode or biometric lock.

8. Your rights

You are in full control of your data at all times. You can create, edit, and permanently delete any note directly within the app. Deleting a note or clearing the app's data removes that information from your device completely.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
